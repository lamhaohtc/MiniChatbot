# Show YouTube video's subtitle/closed caption

You can show YouTube video's subtitles on your OptiSigns Digital Signage player.

![A TED talk playing on screen with its YouTube closed caption shown along the bottom.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/360076614454)

Simply turn on the Caption toggle for your YouTube asset in OptiSigns.
NOTE: The YouTube video needs to have closed caption generated by the video author. Auto-generated captions may not work (this is a YouTube limitation).

![The OptiSigns YouTube app dialog with Name, URL, and the Caption toggle switched on.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55390237542419)
